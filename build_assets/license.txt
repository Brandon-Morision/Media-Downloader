1. NATURE OF THE SOFTWARE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1. Client-Side Utility: NovaDrop is a local-first desktop media extraction and

1.2. No Centralized Media Hosting: NovaDrop does NOT host, cache, re-encode on
