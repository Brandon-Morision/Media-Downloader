## 1. Nature of the Software
1.1. **Client-Side Utility:** NovaDrop is an open-source, local-first desktop media extraction and archival utility designed to retrieve publicly accessible media streams and assets at the explicit direction of the user.  
1.2. **No Central Hosting or Proxying:** NovaDrop does **not** host, store, index, re-encode on remote servers, broadcast, or distribute third-party media content. All network connections and media transfers take place directly between the user's local machine and the public remote servers specified by the user.  
1.3. **Local Bridge Server:** The built-in companion bridge server operates strictly on the local machine's loopback interface (`127.0.0.1:6789`) gated by an ephemeral cryptographic security token. It does not accept remote network connections.
